# Real-match field test

Automated tests cover a lot, but only a real match shows thumb reach, glare, speed and what
you actually want to see. Use the installed app (opened from the Home Screen icon), not Safari.

## Before the match (at home, on wifi)

- [ ] Open the app from the Home Screen icon. The header dot is **green** (signed in and synced).
- [ ] Turn on your phone's large text setting if you normally use it. Nothing should be covered.
- [ ] Tap **Start match**, enter the opponent. The screen should stay awake.
- [ ] Open a share link on another phone, so someone can follow along live.

## During the match

- [ ] Track a full 3-set match (about 120 taps). Everything you need is on one screen with no scrolling.
- [ ] Every tap lands where you meant it to, one-handed. Note any button you mis-tapped.
- [ ] **Undo** removes exactly the last tap. Tap the History to remove an older one.
- [ ] Use the **+ / −** score buttons. **End Set** pre-fills the score.
- [ ] Put the phone in your pocket or lock it for a few minutes, then come back. Nothing was lost.
- [ ] If the gym has no signal, the header says "N to sync" and keeps counting. It clears when you
      are back online. (iPhones only sync while the app is open.)
- [ ] The viewer on the other phone updates within about 20 seconds.

## After the match

- [ ] **Finish match**. Totals on Season and Match details agree with what you saw.
- [ ] Spot check one stat by hand: pass average = (1×ones + 2×twos + 3×threes) ÷ passes;
      hitting % = (kills − errors) ÷ attempts.
- [ ] Open the app on a second device with the same email: same match, same totals.
- [ ] **Copy Text Report** pastes into a message and reads well.
- [ ] Turn off a share link. The viewer says it is no longer active.

## Write down

Anything that was slow, confusing, too small, or in the wrong place. Mistaps matter most, and which
buttons they were. Send the notes and I will turn them into fixes.
